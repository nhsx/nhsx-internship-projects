# New Topic Template

**Keywords:** Causal inference, bayes

**Need:**  Community interventions offer an opportunity to deliver proactive care in advance of advanced and acute needs. This promise results in the rollout of a variety of small-scale programmes. However, the evidence base regarding such interventions are limited and often lack statistical expertise in design precluding analysis through traditional randomisation frameworks.

Counterfactual analysis using casual inference offers an opportunity to quantify the impact of the intervention using observational data. This project aims to identify which community intervention would be most suitable as a case study given the data available upon starting, to estimate the effect of treatment in the treated (ETT) on subsequent healthcare utilisation, and to develop a conceptual workflow to enable the approach to be applied to future initiatives.
